# SWP-50 Seawater Cooling Pump — Operation and Maintenance (fictional sample)

This document is a fictional sample written for testing the assistant. The pump, values and intervals are invented. Do not use it for real equipment.

## Safety

Before any work on the pump: stop the pump, isolate the motor at the main switchboard and apply lockout/tagout, close the suction and discharge valves, and relieve pressure through the drain valve. Seawater may be hot after operation with the engine running.

## Technical data

Flow: 50 m³/h at 3.5 bar. Speed: 2 900 rpm. Motor: 11 kW, 400 V, driven by a VFD-200 frequency converter. Mechanical seal: type MS-28. Impeller material: bronze.

## Start-up

Check that the suction valve is fully open and the pump casing is filled with water. Never run the pump dry: the mechanical seal is damaged within 30 seconds of dry running. Start the motor, then open the discharge valve slowly over about 10 seconds.

## Troubleshooting

Low flow or low discharge pressure: check the sea chest strainer for blockage, check that the suction valve is fully open, check impeller wear.

Abnormal noise or vibration: possible cavitation from a blocked suction strainer, or worn bearings. Measure vibration at the bearing housing; above 4.5 mm/s RMS stop the pump and inspect the bearings.

Leakage at the shaft: a mechanical seal leakage of up to 10 drops per minute is acceptable. Above that, replace the mechanical seal.

## Maintenance schedule

Weekly: clean the sea chest strainer. Every 3 000 hours: check impeller and wear rings, replace the zinc anode. Every 8 000 hours: replace bearings and the mechanical seal. Grease the motor bearings every 2 000 hours with 15 g of marine-grade lithium grease.
